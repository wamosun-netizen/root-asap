About this deal

Lagos Foods Co. imports rice, beans, palm oil and dried fish from verified Nigerian suppliers and distributes to independent African and Caribbean grocers across London and Birmingham. Founded in 2019 by Tunde Adeyemi, the business has grown from a single delivery route to supplying over 60 retailers.

This raise funds a bulk container order timed to lock in pre-harvest pricing, cutting unit costs by an estimated 14% versus spot-buying through the year. Funds are drawn down once the deal closes and repaid from wholesale receivables over the following 18 months.

Yes — this is a fixed-interest deal, so your return doesn't change with sales performance. It's repaid from wholesale receivables regardless of margin on any single shipment.
