How it works

From browsing to your first repayment

Three steps, full visibility at every stage.

1

Answer a few questions

Tell us your goals, risk appetite and the sectors you care about. We match you with relevant deals.

2

Choose a deal, express interest

Review the terms, the business's research, and funding progress, then express interest with an indicative amount. We introduce you directly to the business owner.

3

Agree terms, stay in the loop

Finalise the investment directly with the business owner — Root ASAP never holds your funds — then track progress updates on your dashboard.
