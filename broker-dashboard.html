How staged commission works on Root ASAP

You set your total commission and how it splits across three stages — initiation, process and completion — when you list a deal. Root ASAP never holds or transfers this money: each stage is paid directly by the business owner once its trigger event happens, and this dashboard is where you record and track that it's happened. Marking a stage "Paid" here is a record for you and the business owner, not a payment instruction.
