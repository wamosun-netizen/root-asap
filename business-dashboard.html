How AI matching and introductions work

Matching scores are based on each investor's stated sector, location, ticket size and term preferences against your deal — not a guarantee of investment. Investor identities stay masked until you request an introduction and they accept. Root ASAP never holds your funds or theirs; once introduced, you agree terms and receive funds directly from the investor.
