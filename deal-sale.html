About this business

Dakar Furniture Works designs and builds custom furniture for hotels, restaurants and retail fit-outs across Senegal and the wider region. Founded in 2012 by Amara Diallo, the workshop has grown from a two-person operation to a 14-person team supplying over 30 hospitality and retail clients.

Amara is selling to retire after 13 years running the business, and wants a buyer who will keep the current team on and continue serving existing clients. The sale includes the workshop lease, all equipment, the client book, the brand, and a 3-month handover period with Amara.
